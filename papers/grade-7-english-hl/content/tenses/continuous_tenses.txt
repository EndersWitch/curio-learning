INTRO: The <strong>present continuous tense</strong> shows an action that is happening right now or around now. It is built from <em>am</em>, <em>is</em> or <em>are</em> plus a verb ending in -ing. Choosing the right helping verb and spelling the -ing form correctly are the main skills.

@@CARD
TYPE: definition
TITLE: What is the present continuous?
CONTENT: The present continuous tense shows an action that is in progress at the moment of speaking. It uses am, is or are followed by the -ing form of the verb. The Handbook describes continuous tenses as showing what is happening at the moment or is ongoing.
EXAMPLE: I am driving.<br>Sipho is reading in the library.<br>The learners are writing a test.
@@ENDCARD

@@CARD
TYPE: key_rule
TITLE: Am, is or are
CONTENT: The helping verb must agree with the subject. Use am with I, is with he, she, it and singular nouns, and are with you, we, they and plural nouns. The main verb always ends in -ing.
EXAMPLE: I am cooking.<br>Naledi is cooking.<br>We are cooking.
@@ENDCARD

@@CARD
TYPE: key_rule
TITLE: Spelling the -ing form
CONTENT: Most verbs just add -ing. Verbs ending in a silent e drop the e. Short verbs with one vowel and one final consonant double that consonant. Verbs ending in ie change to ying. Verbs ending in y keep the y.
EXAMPLE: walk → walking<br>make → making<br>run → running<br>swim → swimming<br>lie → lying<br>play → playing
@@ENDCARD

@@CARD
TYPE: common_mistake
TITLE: Verbs that rarely take -ing
CONTENT: Verbs that describe states, such as know, like, want, love and have (meaning own), are not normally used in the continuous. We say I know the answer, not I am knowing the answer. Leaving out the helping verb is also a mistake.
EXAMPLE: Wrong: I am knowing the answer.<br>Wrong: Sipho reading a book.<br>Right: I know the answer.<br>Right: Sipho is reading a book.
@@ENDCARD

@@CARD
TYPE: try_it
TITLE: Complete the sentence
CONTENT: Write the correct present continuous. The children (play) outside at the moment.
EXAMPLE: The children are playing outside at the moment. Children is plural, so the helping verb is are. Play keeps its y and adds -ing.
@@ENDCARD

TESTED:
Form the present continuous with am, is and are
Match the helping verb to the subject
Spell the -ing form of common verbs
Recognise signal words such as now and at the moment
Know that state verbs such as know and like are not used in the continuous
@@ENDLEVEL

INTRO: The <strong>past continuous tense</strong> describes an action that was in progress at a time before now. It uses <em>was</em> or <em>were</em> plus an -ing verb. It is often paired with the simple past to show one action interrupting another.

@@CARD
TYPE: definition
TITLE: What is the past continuous?
CONTENT: The past continuous tense shows an ongoing action at a time that has finished. It uses was or were with the -ing form of the verb. The Handbook example is I was driving.
EXAMPLE: I was driving.<br>Thabo was reading at eight o'clock last night.<br>The children were sleeping.
@@ENDCARD

@@CARD
TYPE: key_rule
TITLE: Was or were
CONTENT: Use was with I, he, she, it and singular nouns. Use were with you, we, they and plural nouns. The verb ending in -ing stays the same. Negatives use was not or were not, and the short forms are wasn't and weren't.
EXAMPLE: She was walking.<br>They were walking.<br>We weren't walking.
@@ENDCARD

@@CARD
TYPE: example
TITLE: Interrupted actions
CONTENT: The past continuous often sets the scene, and the simple past gives the action that interrupted it. When joins the two parts, and while joins two actions that were going on at the same time.
EXAMPLE: We were walking home when it started to rain.<br>While Mia was cooking, Riaan was setting the table.
@@ENDCARD

@@CARD
TYPE: spot_difference
TITLE: Simple past and past continuous
SIDE_A: Simple past
SIDE_B: Past continuous
CONTENT: Finished action | Action in progress<br>Lerato read a book. | Lerato was reading a book.<br>It happened and ended. | It was going on at that time.
EXAMPLE: Lerato was reading when I arrived. Reading was in progress, and arrived was the short action that happened in the middle of it.
@@ENDCARD

@@CARD
TYPE: common_mistake
TITLE: Was and were must agree
CONTENT: Mixing up was and were is a very common error. A plural subject needs were. Using the simple past form after was or were is also wrong.
EXAMPLE: Wrong: The learners was writing.<br>Wrong: Sipho was wrote a letter.<br>Right: The learners were writing.<br>Right: Sipho was writing a letter.
@@ENDCARD

TESTED:
Form the past continuous with was and were
Use when and while with the past continuous
Identify the interrupting action in a sentence
Choose between the simple past and the past continuous
Make agreement between was or were and the subject
@@ENDLEVEL

INTRO: The <strong>future continuous tense</strong> shows an action that will be in progress at a time in the future. It is built from <em>will be</em> plus an -ing verb. It can sound like the simple future, but the meaning is different.

@@CARD
TYPE: definition
TITLE: What is the future continuous?
CONTENT: The future continuous tense describes an action that will be going on at a stated future time. It uses will be (or shall be with I and we) followed by the -ing form of the verb. The Handbook example is I shall be driving.
EXAMPLE: I shall be driving to the coast tomorrow.<br>At eight tomorrow, Naledi will be writing her test.<br>They will be waiting at the gate.
@@ENDCARD

@@CARD
TYPE: key_rule
TITLE: Will be plus -ing
CONTENT: Both parts are needed. Will comes first, then be, then the -ing verb. Will be never changes for different subjects. Negatives use will not be or won't be. Questions put will first.
EXAMPLE: She will be working.<br>They won't be working.<br>Will you be working?
@@ENDCARD

@@CARD
TYPE: spot_difference
TITLE: Simple future and future continuous
SIDE_A: Simple future
SIDE_B: Future continuous
CONTENT: The action will happen | The action will be in progress<br>I will drive to the coast. | I will be driving to the coast.<br>A single event | An ongoing action at a set time
EXAMPLE: Tomorrow at ten I will be driving to the coast. This tells us what I will be in the middle of at that time.
@@ENDCARD

@@CARD
TYPE: common_mistake
TITLE: Do not drop be
CONTENT: The word be is the link between will and the -ing verb. Leaving it out, or writing will being, gives a wrong form. The verb after be must end in -ing.
EXAMPLE: Wrong: She will working.<br>Wrong: She will being working.<br>Wrong: She will be work.<br>Right: She will be working.
@@ENDCARD

@@CARD
TYPE: try_it
TITLE: Say what will be happening
CONTENT: Write the future continuous. This time next week, I (swim) in the sea at Durban.
EXAMPLE: This time next week, I will be swimming in the sea at Durban. Swim doubles the m before -ing.
@@ENDCARD

TESTED:
Form the future continuous with will be and an -ing verb
Tell the simple future from the future continuous
Make negatives and questions in the future continuous
Spot missing or extra words in the verb group
Use time phrases such as this time tomorrow
@@ENDLEVEL

A: The children was playing in the yard.
B: The children were playing in the yard.
C: The children were play in the yard.
D: The children playing in the yard.
CORRECT: B
EXPLAIN:
Children is plural, so were is the helping verb, and playing is the -ing form. A uses was with a plural subject. C leaves off the -ing ending and D has no helping verb.
