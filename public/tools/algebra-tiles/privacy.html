Privacy

What we collect, what we don’t, and why.

Algebra Tiles does not collect personal information from teachers or students. There are no accounts and nothing to sign in to. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to anonymously count the number of visits, which helps us understand how Algebra Tiles is being used in classrooms. Cloudflare Web Analytics is cookieless, does not fingerprint visitors, and does not track users across other sites; see Cloudflare’s privacy policy for details. We do not share, sell, or otherwise transfer any visitor data to third parties.

Nothing a teacher or student does here is saved by us. A problem lives only in its link, and a student’s tiles and answers stay in their own browser, gone when they close the page.

Questions or concerns? Email support@teacher.dev.
