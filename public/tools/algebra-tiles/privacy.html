Privacy

What we collect, what we don’t, and why.

Algebra Tiles does not collect personal information from teachers or students. There are no accounts and nothing to sign in to. This page uses no cookies or trackers.

Nothing a teacher or student does here is saved by us. A problem lives only in its link, and a student’s tiles and answers stay in their own browser, gone when they close the page.

Questions or concerns? Email hello@curiolearning.co.za.
