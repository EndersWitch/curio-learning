INTRO: Some words mean almost the same thing and some mean the opposite. Words with similar meanings are <strong>synonyms</strong> and words with opposite meanings are <strong>antonyms</strong>.

@@CARD
TYPE: definition
TITLE: Synonyms
CONTENT: Synonyms are words of the same or similar meaning. They are often interchangeable, but the best choice depends on the context.
EXAMPLE: big, large<br>happy, cheerful
@@ENDCARD

@@CARD
TYPE: definition
TITLE: Antonyms
CONTENT: Antonyms are words of opposite meaning.
EXAMPLE: hot, cold<br>modern, old-fashioned
@@ENDCARD

TITLE: The thesaurus
CONTENT: A thesaurus is the best source of reference for synonyms. A dictionary gives meanings and spelling.

INTRO: <strong>Synonyms</strong> are words with the same or similar meaning, and <strong>antonyms</strong> are words with opposite meanings. This Grade 7 revision uses the harder words you meet in exams.

@@CARD
TYPE: definition
TITLE: Synonyms
CONTENT: Synonyms are words of the same or similar meaning. They are often interchangeable, but the best choice depends on the context. A thesaurus is the best source of synonyms.
EXAMPLE: brave, courageous<br>huge, enormous<br>calm, tranquil<br>scarce, rare
@@ENDCARD

@@CARD
TYPE: definition
TITLE: Antonyms
CONTENT: Antonyms are words of opposite meaning. An antonym may be a different word, or it may be made by adding a prefix or a suffix.
EXAMPLE: modern, old-fashioned<br>accept, reject<br>useful, useless<br>pro-exams, anti-exams
@@ENDCARD

@@CARD
TYPE: key_rule
TITLE: Useful pairs to know
CONTENT: These pairs appear often. Learn both words in each pair.
EXAMPLE: abundance, scarcity<br>compulsory, optional<br>humane, cruel<br>comedy, tragedy<br>include, exclude
@@ENDCARD

@@CARD
TYPE: common_mistake
TITLE: Not always interchangeable
CONTENT: Two synonyms may mean almost the same thing, but one may sound wrong in a sentence. Check that the word fits the context before you swap it.
EXAMPLE: She was brave. She was courageous.<br>They are both fine, but a courageous firefighter sounds stronger than a brave firefighter.
@@ENDCARD
