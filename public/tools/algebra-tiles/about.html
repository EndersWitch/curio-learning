Algebra Tiles

About

A free tool for multiplying and factoring polynomials with algebra tiles.

Part of curio learning

Algebra Tiles is part of curio learning, a free CAPS-aligned learning site for South African learners in Grades 4 to 12. It is adapted from open-source software; see the licences page.
