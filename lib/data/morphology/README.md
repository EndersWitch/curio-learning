Word-part data from teacher.dev's Morphology Mash (Apache-2.0, https://github.com/teacherdotdev/morphology-mash).

- `morphemes.json`: 629 prefixes, suffixes and roots, each with meanings, a grade band and an origin.
- `words.json`: 2,466 words, each with a definition, a grade and its split into parts.

The spelling is American (colors, realize, recognize). Convert to South African English before it reaches a learner. The grade bands are US grades, not CAPS grades.
