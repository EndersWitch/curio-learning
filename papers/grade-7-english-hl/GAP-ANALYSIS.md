# Grade 7 English HL: gap analysis against what curio already teaches

Compared the 30 planned Grade 7 subtopics with the live Grade 5 and Grade 6 English levels (names and descriptions only, not the questions themselves). Grade 4 was not compared. Verdicts:

- REPEAT: Grade 6 already teaches the same skills.
- PARTLY NEW: mostly a repeat, with a few Grade 7 additions.
- NEW: nothing like it in Grades 5 or 6.

## Result

- Of 30 planned subtopics, 4 are fully new, 2 are deeper, and the other 24 are repeats or mostly repeats.
- Remaining planned: 68 levels, 714 questions. Genuinely new material is roughly 14 levels and 150 questions.

## Suggested plan for the rest

1. Write the new material in full: borrowed words, one word for a phrase, persuasive language, bias and stereotypes, concord (about 14 levels, 146 questions).
2. Add homonyms and the Grade 7 additions inside repeated topics (infinitive, abbreviations already live).
3. For each repeated topic, write one Grade 7 exam-style revision level instead of 3 to 5 levels (about 12 levels, 170 questions).
4. Decide what to do with the 42 repeat levels already live: keep, hide, or merge.
