## Added after reading the last four papers

- WC25-T2 (official WCED, 20 marks of language) tests: abstract nouns, antonyms, sentence types, joining sentences with a conjunction, concord, singular/plural nouns, prepositions, completing a proverb, idiom meaning and use, subject/verb/object, punctuation, possessive pronouns.
- WC25-T1 (official WCED, 10 marks): superlative, punctuation (apostrophe), joining sentences, concord, sentence type, antonym.
- ANA15 (official DBE): noun identification, subject and predicate, inverted commas, prefix to make an antonym, joining sentences with a conjunction, synonym, rewrite in the present tense, figure of speech (x2) plus explaining it.
- BL-M19: subject/predicate/object, active to passive, nine-tense table, main and subordinate clause, parts of speech, figure of speech (one is an advert: "Coca-Cola is a ball").
- New topics from these: proverbs, prefixes for antonyms, joining sentences with conjunctions, inverted commas, possessive pronouns, superlative. The official WCED and DBE papers confirm: concord, antonyms/synonyms, sentence types, idioms and figures of speech, subject/verb/object, punctuation.
- Heavy tense tables (9 and 12 tenses) appear only in the two Blouberg papers, so treat them as school-specific until the ATP confirms.
