INTRO: In the <strong>active voice</strong> the subject does the action. In the <strong>passive voice</strong> the subject receives it. This Grade 7 revision checks that you can recognise both and change one into the other.

@@CARD
TYPE: spot_difference
TITLE: Active and passive
SIDE_A: Voice
SIDE_B: Example
CONTENT: Active, the subject performs the action | The boy kicks the ball.<br>Passive, the subject receives the action | The ball is kicked by the boy.
EXAMPLE: The object of the active sentence becomes the subject of the passive one.
@@ENDCARD

@@CARD
TYPE: step_by_step
TITLE: Active to passive
CONTENT: 1. Find the subject, verb and object.<br>2. Move the object to the front.<br>3. Use the correct tense of be plus the past participle.<br>4. Add by and the original subject, changing pronouns to the object form.
EXAMPLE: The boy kicked the ball.<br>The ball was kicked by the boy.
@@ENDCARD

@@CARD
TYPE: key_rule
TITLE: Tenses and pronouns
CONTENT: The form of be shows the tense. Subject pronouns change to object pronouns after by. Only sentences with a transitive verb, one that takes a direct object, can be made passive.
EXAMPLE: is kicked, was kicked, will be kicked<br>He completed the form. The form was completed by him.
@@ENDCARD

@@CARD
TYPE: key_rule
TITLE: Why use the passive
CONTENT: The passive is used when the doer is unknown, unimportant or obvious. The agent can then be left out.
EXAMPLE: The diamonds were stolen.<br>Refreshments will be served.<br>Smoking is not permitted in the cinema.
@@ENDCARD

Which is the passive form of The boy kicked the ball?
A: The ball is kicked by the boy.
B: The ball was kicked by the boy.
C: The ball will be kicked by the boy.
D: The boy was kicked by the ball.
CORRECT: B
EXPLAIN:
The tense stays in the simple past, so was kicked is used. The other versions change the tense or swap the roles.
