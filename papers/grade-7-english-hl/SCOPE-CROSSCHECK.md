## Findings that change the plan

1. **Tenses.** CAPS lists the full tense set for the whole of Gr 7-9. The Grade 7 ATP names only simple present and simple past, but two Grade 7 papers (Blouberg mid-year and final 2019) test the full table, and the Handbook has a dedicated 3x3 section (simple/continuous/perfect x present/past/future, around lines 993-1002 of the text). You've chosen all nine. Perfect continuous tenses (for example "I have been driving") are not in your list, so they stay out. Worth knowing: the ATP alone would have left six of the nine out, so this is wider than the official Grade 7 plan.
2. **Pun, ambiguity, sexist language, irony, hyperbole, euphemism, cliche.** These are in the CAPS vocabulary list for Gr 7-9. The Grade 7 ATP names only bias, prejudice and stereotypes (T2, T3). They appeared in a paper (BL-F19: pun, sexism, ambiguity). Your call whether they go in. They fit your "figures of speech and stereotypes etc." request.
3. **Phase-wide CAPS items not in the Grade 7 ATP** (for example conditionals, moods, noun clauses, modals, gerunds, ideophones) are out of scope. Say if you want any added.
