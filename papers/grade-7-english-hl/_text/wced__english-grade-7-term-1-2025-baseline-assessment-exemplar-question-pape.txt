Text A  
Read the story and then answer the questions that follow.  
 
 
1. Who was the leader of the dinosaurs? Please answer in a full sentence.             (1) 
 
 
1. A long time ago, in a long-lost land of forests, atop a high mountain, trapped in an 
ancient volcano, lived the last group of big, angry dinosaurs. For thousands of years, 
they had survived all the changes on earth.  
 
2. Now they decided to leave their safe hiding place, under the leadership of the great 
Tyrannosaurus Rex. Dinosaurs had been separated from the world for far too long, and 
it was time for them to reclaim their power. When their leader told them of this plan, 
the dinosaurs’ roars were as loud as thunder. 
 
 
3. They began to break down the walls of the great crater, so that they could reach the 
outside world. When they were done, all the dinosaurs sharpened their claws, ready to 
terrorize the world again. 
 
4. But everything outside the volcano was very different from what the dinosaurs 
remembered. For days they journeyed through the mountains. Finally, from the top of 
one mountain, they saw a village down in the valley. It was so far away from them that 
the houses and people seemed very small.  
 
 
5. They began charging down the mountain, ready to crush anything that stood in their 
way...But the closer they got to the village, the bigger the houses seemed. When the 
dinosaurs finally reached the village, the houses were much bigger than the dinosaurs 
themselves. A young boy walked by and cried to his father, "Daddy! Daddy! Just look 
at those little dinosaurs! Can we take them home?" 
 
6. And so it goes in life… The fearsome Tyrannosaurus Rex and his friends became the 
pets of the village children. After millions of years of evolution, they had evolved into a 
miniature species. The dinosaurs learned that nothing lasts forever, and that one must 
always be ready to adapt to meet the challenges that lie ahead. 
Source: Adapted from DBE Workbook, Grade 6 (Afrikaans), Volume 2
